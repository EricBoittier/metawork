Periodic (Ewald) systems from a peer session (metawork-54). Sections 1–6 are the original investigation of a real, large discrepancy; section 7 onward is the root-cause fix and the now-exact validated results (AuMgO and bio_dimers both at the float32 noise floor vs. JAX, bio_dimers matching the paper's own reported accuracy).
18 · Cumulene / bio_dimers restart results cumulene matches paper
PDF
Follow-up to notebook 16: after the exclusion_radius fix, restarts training on cumulene and bio_dimers with tuned (lowered) learning rates to see whether ordinary fine-tuning recovers the paper's own reported test-set accuracy. Cumulene fully closes the gap (matches RMSE, beats MAE, on the full test set); bio_dimers improves substantially but stays short. Also documents a real methodology trap: a fixed, non-random 300-frame monitoring slice was misleading in opposite directions for the two systems.

19 · Liquid ethanol NVT: PET-MAD via LAMMPS
PDF
From a peer session: a 700-molecule liquid-density ethanol box (6300 atoms), 200 ps NVT with PET-MAD through LAMMPS's pair_style metatomic. Thermostat/energy/pressure traces and O-O/O-H/C-C RDFs confirm a stable, physically sane liquid run — a real condensed-phase stress test beyond the single-molecule vacuum case in notebook 17. The LOREM half of the original request (no H/C/O checkpoint available) and the full 1 ns target were not completed; documented honestly as partial.
